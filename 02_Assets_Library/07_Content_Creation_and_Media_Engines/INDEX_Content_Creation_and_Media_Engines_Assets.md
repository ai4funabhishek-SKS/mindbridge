# Asset Library Index: Domain 07 — Content Creation and Media Engines

## Overview
This directory catalogs all raw and structured content creation, media synthesis, YouTube automation, generative video, graphic design, and social media repurposing assets.

## Synthesized Golden Master Playbooks (`03_Topics_and_Projects/07_Content_Creation_and_Media_Engines/`)
1. **`GM_Faceless_YouTube_Production_Engine_and_Script_Generation.md`**: Complete faceless YouTube video pipeline covering competitor outlier analysis, retention scriptwriting, ElevenLabs TTS, and FFmpeg video assembly.
2. **`GM_Generative_AI_Video_and_Avatar_Cloning_Workflows.md`**: Google Veo 3 cinematic video prompting, Hedra AI talking avatar lip-sync, and automated news anchor synthesis.
3. **`GM_Automated_Graphic_Design_and_YouTube_Thumbnail_Engines.md`**: High-CTR thumbnail formulas, Python Pillow text overlay engine, and automated visual contrast optimization.
4. **`GM_Omnichannel_Social_Media_Syndication_and_Repurposing.md`**: 1-to-many repurposing engine converting long-form assets into X threads, LinkedIn posts/carousels, Instagram reels, and Telegram broadcasts.
5. **`GM_SEO_Blog_Generation_and_Content_Strategy_Playbook.md`**: Google Helpful Content-compliant SEO blog synthesis, `yt-dlp` transcription pipelines, and editorial calendar management.
